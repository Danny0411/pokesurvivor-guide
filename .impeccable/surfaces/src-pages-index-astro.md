# Surface: PokéSurvivor 完全圖鑑 (whole site shell, home, dex, Pokémon pages, reference chapters)

Mode: Operate (lookup tool), with the chapter pages read as Read. Audience: Taiwanese players looking things up mid-run on a phone, plus long desk reads. Content: only game facts (PRODUCT.md).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a searchable 完全攻略本. It has colored thumb-index tabs on the book edge, dense ruled spec tables in chapter colors, and one full spread per Pokémon with an annotated figure. It refuses two category defaults: the dark neon "gamer" dashboard and the white card-grid Pokédex clone.

OWN-WORLD: Bright coated-paper white ground and true ink black. Every chapter owns one process color (red, cyan, yellow, green, magenta, orange, violet). That color appears as the edge tab, the solid table header band, and an 8% row tint. Halftone dot screens shade the section heads. The 18 type colors are a fixed language and never decorate. One reserved selection color marks "current". All numbers share one fixed stepped ramp. The face is a heavy Taiwanese gothic for heads, with condensed tabular numerals for data. Sprites always render at integer pixel scale on an 8px cell grid.

STORY: The visitor finds any game fact in one search or two taps, trusts it because the mechanics and formula sit beside the number, and shares the page link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The home page is a contents spread. On the left is the site name, a big search field and the "966" count. On the right is the chapter list with tab colors and entry counts. The edge tabs run down the right side on desktop and across the bottom on mobile. A frieze of real sprites walks along the bottom.

SIGNATURE: On each Pokémon spread, the figure plays the sprite (8-direction dial, 7 actions, shiny toggle). SVG leader lines draw from the figure to callout labels. Sorting a dex table reranks rows in place (FLIP).

FORM: 完全攻略本 (Japanese complete guidebook), pick card, 1st on my ordered list; seed key 7e355f4c.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
